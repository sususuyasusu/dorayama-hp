Concept

Concept

Each dorayaki is baked by hand on a copper griddle every morning,and has long been offered to Tomioka Hachimangu Shrine.

Offered to Tomioka Hachimangu,baked one by one each morning.The kind you want again,the kind you want every day.

Dorayama is a dorayaki shop in Monzen-nakacho, near the approach that links Fukagawa Fudodo and Tomioka Hachimangu Shrine. Every morning we bake our auspicious “Monnaka dorayaki”, which we offer to Tomioka Hachimangu, in the shop. Fluffy pancakes and red bean paste with a touch of salt. Warm one up and the freshly baked aroma comes back — a dorayaki for everyday treats and for gifts.

  1. 01

    Futon batter

    We separate the eggs and whip the whites into a fine meringue before folding it into the batter. With plenty of air, the pancakes bake up light like sponge cake — soft and plump like a futon.

  2. 02

    Hand-baked on copper

    Every morning the batter is baked by hand, one at a time, on the copper griddle at the front of the shop. Copper spreads heat evenly, and we turn each pancake while watching its colour. The smell of fresh baking drifts out to the street.

  3. 03

    A new “san-dō-wari”

    “San-dō-wari” is the traditional wagashi ratio of equal parts egg, flour and sugar. Building on it through thousands of trials, we arrived at a golden ratio of our own.

  4. 04

    Salted red bean paste

    Red beans from Tokachi, Hokkaido are cooked with white sugar into a lightly sweet paste. A hint of salt and whole beans left in give it texture — a contrast with the soft pancake.

  5. 05

    Japanese ingredients

    Tokachi red beans, pure Japanese hon-mirin, Kagawa wheat flour, Japanese rice bran oil and Hokkaido beet sugar. We choose Japanese ingredients for a taste you will not tire of, even every day.

Store

On the approach,freshly baked

On the approach to Fukagawa Fudodo and Tomioka Hachimangu.We bake the pancakes in the shop every morning.

Our main shop opened in Monzen-nakacho in June 2022. One minute from Monzen-nakacho Station on the Tokyo Metro Tozai Line, it is a small shop with a white noren curtain in the side street behind the MUFG Bank building. Through the window you can watch us bake on the copper griddle, and the smell of fresh baking greets you at the door. We hope you will drop in after visiting the shrine, or for a small gift.

Access

A one-minute walk from Monzen-nakacho Station,open every day from 10:00 until sold out.

Reservations are taken on Take Eats (in Japanese) for pick-up from the next day onward, from 5 pieces, up to 30 days ahead (pick-up 10:30–15:45). You can pay online or in the shop; in the shop we accept credit cards and transit IC cards.
